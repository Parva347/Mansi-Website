# Mansi Industries Brand Documentation

# 1. Company Overview

Mansi Industries is a premium Indian manufacturer specializing in 100% melamine tableware for commercial and hospitality applications.

The company manufactures high-quality melamine products designed for hotels, restaurants, caterers, dealers, distributors and institutional buyers throughout India.

The website represents the company itself rather than functioning as an ecommerce store. Its purpose is to establish trust, showcase manufacturing capabilities, present the product catalogue professionally and generate qualified wholesale enquiries.

# 7. Website Objectives

The website exists to:

Present Mansi Industries professionally.

Showcase the complete product catalogue.

Build confidence in product quality.

Generate qualified wholesale enquiries.

Allow customers to browse products easily.

Enable buyers to request quotations quickly.

Provide a premium digital experience.

The website does NOT exist to:

Sell products online.

Process payments.

Support retail purchases.

Offer single-piece ordering.

Certifications

Manufacturing statistics
